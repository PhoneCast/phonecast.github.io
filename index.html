Phone as a source

Scan. It's in.

Open PhoneCast on your PC, scan the QR with the phone app and your phone shows up as a source. No plugins, no cables. Video goes straight from the phone to your PC over your local network.

Phone mode

No PC needed.

Prefer to leave the PC off? Stream directly from the phone app to Twitch, YouTube or Kick, with your screen, the phone's audio and your microphone.

Floating chat

Chat over your game.

Emotes, badges, alerts and quick messages float above your game, and you run the stream from there: pause, “Be right back” and end. With “Single app” the chat never shows up in the stream.

Twitch alerts

Alerts are just another source.

Follows, subs and cheers are built in. No outside services, no browser sources.

Private by design

Your key stays yours.

Sign in and your stream key is fetched automatically. Everything is encrypted on your device, with no servers of ours in the middle.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

?
Is PhoneCast for PC or for phone?

Both. There is a PC program and a phone app. Stream straight from your PC, bring your phone into the PC as a source by scanning a QR, or stream from the phone alone.

Do I need a PC to stream?

No. Phone mode streams straight from your phone to Twitch, YouTube or Kick, with your screen, the phone's audio and your microphone.

How does my phone connect to my PC?

Open PhoneCast on your PC, scan the QR with the phone app and the phone appears as a source. The video goes straight from the phone to your PC over your local network. No plugins and no cables.

Is my stream key safe?

Your stream key is fetched automatically when you sign in, and everything is stored encrypted on your device. None of it goes through servers of ours.

Will chat show up in my stream?

Chat floats over your game for you to read, with emotes, badges, alerts and quick messages. In “Single app” mode it does not appear in the live stream.

Which platforms and devices work?

Twitch, YouTube, Kick and others. Today PhoneCast is available for Windows (the PC program) and for Android as an APK (the phone app). Twitch alerts are integrated as a source.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is PhoneCast safe?

That message appears for any new program that does not have a digital signature yet. PhoneCast's installer is not signed yet because signing has a yearly cost. To continue, click More info, then Run anyway. The components it uses are listed on the Licenses page.
