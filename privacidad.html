Privacy policy

In short: we do not collect your data.

What is stored and where

Your sessions (Twitch, YouTube, Kick) and your stream keys are stored encrypted only on your computer, with Windows, and on your phone, with the Android Keystore. We do not send them to any server of ours.

Where the video goes

The video travels from your phone to your PC over your local network, and from there straight to the platform you choose. In phone mode it goes straight from the phone to the platform.

Connections we do make

The app checks GitHub for updates, and the installer downloads FFmpeg from its official site. Those connections carry none of your data. When this page loads, your browser downloads fonts from Google Fonts. When you sign in to Twitch, YouTube or Kick, that part is handled by them, under their own policies.
